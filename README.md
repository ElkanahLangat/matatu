# matatu
A passenger-first Nairobi Matatu platform that combines 
booking, 
real-time vehicle tracking, 
adaptive arrival predictions, 
offline tickets, 
safety insights, 
and commuter behavior data to make everyday public transport more predictable.


_Description_

Building a smart, passenger-centered Matatu mobility platform that makes commuting in Nairobi more predictable, convenient, and trustworthy.

The system allows passengers to discover and book available Matatus, compare fares and estimated arrival times, pay digitally, receive offline booking receipts, and use unique identifiers when boarding. 
Passengers receives real-time updates about vehicle locations, traffic delays, pickup points, routes, and safer drop-off options, allowing them to spend less time waiting in crowded stages such as Kencom, GPO, and Archives.

The platform also connects passengers with conductors and operators, helping them understand demand and communicate with passengers before and during trips; seamlessly. 
Through simple, engaging interactions, the system learns about commuting patterns, delays, safety concerns, and passenger preferences while avoiding unnecessary collection of sensitive information.
This connects more trust with the passenger in everyday psychological conditions

The long-term goal is not simply to create another booking app, but to build a mobility system that understands how Nairobi actually does the following:
- moves and uses real-time information,
- sheng'language interactions,
- commuter behavior,
- and intelligent predictions to make everyday transportation more
                                       - connected,
                                       - safe, and
                                       - trusted.
